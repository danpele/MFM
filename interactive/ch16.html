Digital Assets and Their Market

Five Coins, One Dominant Asset

    Question for the room What share of the value of the five coins does Bitcoin hold, and how stable has this share been since 2018?
    Answer

    82.5% on 18 September 2026. The share ranged between 55.8% (1 Feb 2018) and 88.5% (5 Sep 2019). Ethereum holds 16.2%, and Dogecoin, Cardano and Litecoin together less than 1.5%. The total value, 1,970 billion USD, follows prices, because supply changes slowly.

    Stylised Facts of Crypto Returns

    Volatility Over Time

      Question for the room How volatile is Bitcoin today compared with the S&P 500?
      Answer

      On the one-year window, the annualised volatility is 45% for Bitcoin and 13% for the S&P 500, so Bitcoin is 3.5 times as volatile. Bitcoin volatility fell from 88% to 45%, and Ethereum volatility from 109% to 64%. The decline is consistent with a maturing market, but the chart does not identify a cause.

      A 7-Day Market with a Weekend Lull

        Question for the room Is Bitcoin as volatile at weekends as on weekdays?
        Answer

        No. The ratio of the weekend variance to the weekday variance is 0.45 before 11 January 2024 and 0.39 after. At weekends there is no macroeconomic news, no US stock market and no ETF flows. All days are calmer after the ETF launch: on weekdays, the root mean squared return falls from 4.01% to 2.73%.

        Is Bitcoin Moving with Shares?

          Question for the room Did the correlation between Bitcoin and the S&P 500 change at the spot ETF launch or earlier?
          Answer

          Earlier. The correlation is 0.02 in 2017–2019, 0.39 in 2020–2023 and 0.38 after the ETF launch. The break appears in early 2020; the sup-Wald test of the lecture dates it to 9 March 2020. With gold, the correlation rises from −0.02 to 0.12 and then to 0.22.

          Crypto Indices: CRIX

          Crypto Indices Since 2018

            Question for the room The equally weighted index had a compound annual return of 30.0%, above the 21.4% of the cap-weighted index. Is this a stable premium?
            Answer

            No. The difference comes from 2021, when the equally weighted index rose by 998% and the cap-weighted index by 113%. Its volatility is also higher: 82.5% against 65.7%. The five coins are chosen ex post among today's survivors, so both returns are biased upwards (survivorship bias).

            Stablecoins

            Deviations from 1 USD

              Question for the room How often does the closing price of a stablecoin deviate from 1 USD by more than 50 bp?
              Answer

              On less than 1% of the days in 2021–2026: 0.14% for Tether and USD Coin, 0.57% for Dai. The standard deviation of the close is 7.3 bp, 7.5 bp and 10.8 bp respectively. Stress episodes are rare but severe: the daily low of USD Coin was −1,226 bp, on 11 March 2023.

              March 2023: a Bank Failure Hits USD Coin

                Question for the room Silicon Valley Bank was closed on Friday, 10 March 2023. Why did USD Coin reach its low on Saturday?
                Answer

                At the weekend redemptions could not be settled, so the arbitrage that keeps the price within the band disappeared. The daily low of USD Coin was 0.8774 USD on Saturday, 11 March, with a close of 0.9715 USD. Dai, backed by crypto-assets, fell to 0.8970 USD, while Tether stayed above the peg.

                DeFi and Automated Market Makers

                Total Value Locked in DeFi

                  Question for the room How much of the monthly variation of TVL does the Ether price explain?
                  Answer

                  In the regression of monthly log changes, β = 0.79 (Newey–West SE 0.07) and R² = 0.62, over 80 months. TVL is the value of the deposited tokens, so it moves mechanically with their prices. TVL rose from 0.6 billion USD in January 2020 to 177 billion on 9 Nov 2021 and is 88 billion today.

                  A Liquidity Provider in an Ether Pool

                    Question for the room Ether ended the period close to its initial price (r = 1.11). Did the liquidity provider lose against simply holding?
                    Answer

                    Very little: 105.4 against 105.5, an impermanent loss of −0.14%. Against the rebalancing benchmark (122.4), however, the gap is 17.0 points. The realised LVR is 5.83% a year, close to σ²/8 = 5.79% (σ = 68.1%), so fees must bring about 5.8% a year to match the benchmark.

                    Spot ETFs and Crypto Equities

                    IBIT Follows Bitcoin

                      Question for the room How much does IBIT lose per year against Bitcoin?
                      Answer

                      The log of the IBIT/Bitcoin ratio falls by 0.24% a year, close to the annual fee of 0.25%. On 18 September 2026, IBIT stands at 172.8 and Bitcoin at 174.5 (both 100 on 11 Jan 2024). ETHA has the same drift, −0.24% a year.

                      Daily vs Weekly Returns and the Dimson Beta

                        Question for the room Why is the R² between IBIT and Bitcoin 0.82 on daily returns but 0.98 on weekly returns?
                        Answer

                        The daily close of the coin and the New York close are several hours apart, so part of the move of day t reaches the ETF on day t ± 1. On weekly returns the offset matters little: beta goes from 0.93 to 1.01. Newey–West standard errors do not correct the bias of beta.

                        Are Cryptos Becoming Alternative Assets?

                        Cryptos Among Other Asset Classes

                          Question for the room Did crypto-assets move closer to the other asset classes between W1 (2019 – June 2021) and W2 (January 2024 – September 2026)?
                          Answer

                          The point estimates suggest so: the distance to equities falls from 3.03 to 2.63, and to commodities from 2.26 to 1.86. Crypto-assets have also become more alike: their spread around their centre falls from 2.13 to 1.43. Bitcoin volatility falls from 77% to 47%.

                            AI for Scientific Discovery

                            Mini-Case: Did the ETFs Break the Correlation?

                              Question for the room If we let the data choose the break date, is it 11 January 2024?
                              Answer

                              No. The largest z is on 5 February 2020 (z = 7.26). At the ETF date z = −1.48, and 82% of the candidate dates give a larger |z|. The ±1.96 band is pointwise: the maximum over more than a thousand overlapping dates needs its own critical value, which is why inference uses the sup-Wald test.

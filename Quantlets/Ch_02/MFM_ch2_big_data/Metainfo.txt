Name of QuantLet: 'MFM_ch2_big_data'

Published in: 'Modelling Financial Markets (MFM)'

Description: 'Case study, Martin and Nagel (2022), Market efficiency in the age of big data: the specification of their Sec. VII applied to the 49 US industry portfolios from July 1926. Predictors: simple and squared returns at lags 2 to 120 months (238 predictors), demeaned and standardised across portfolios each month, equal weight per month. Rolling 20-year OLS gives the in-sample return r_IS and the out-of-sample portfolio return r_OOS (10-year moving average with two-standard-error bands, as in their Fig. VIII); a full-sample ridge regression from 1971 with leave-one-year-out cross-validation gives the coefficients by lag (Fig. VII).'

Keywords: 'market efficiency, big data, Bayesian learning, in-sample vs out-of-sample predictability, ridge regression, cross-validation, momentum, long-run reversal, momentum seasonality, industry portfolios'

Description: 'Equal-weighted, daily rebalanced portfolios of US, European and Romanian banks, 2010-2026, with the largest peak-to-trough falls and annualised volatilities.

Returns are measured between consecutive dates common to all banks and indices, after aligning prices. A gap can therefore span more than one trading day. Constituent returns are in local currencies without FX conversion; the European basket mixes currencies. The value 100 is invested before the first displayed return; the first point already includes that return. peak_date is the all-time index maximum, not the peak preceding the largest drawdown. vol is the standard deviation of the equal-weighted mean log return, scaled by √252, a 252-observation convention on this common calendar. The wealth curve instead compounds the equal-weighted simple return. The Romanian basket contains TLV and BRD.'

Name of QuantLet: 'MFM_ch15_lookahead'

Published in: 'Modelling Financial Markets (MFM)'

Description: 'Does a language model already know what markets did? Qwen2.5 models are asked, without any context, whether the S&P 500 rose or fell in each month since 2000 and whether Apple rose or fell on single days; AUC before and after the release of the weights.

The post-release monthly sample contains only 23 observations, and all its reported AUC intervals include 0.5. In the daily task, some models choose almost the same class every time; an AUC can still rank small differences in their probabilities while balanced accuracy stays near 0.5. These diagnostics alone do not establish useful trading predictability or identify a cause of pre/post differences.'

Keywords: 'look-ahead bias, memorisation, large language model, knowledge cutoff, AUC, backtesting'

Author: 'Daniel Traian Pele'

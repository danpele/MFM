Name of QuantLet: 'MFM_ch15_embeddings'

Published in: 'Modelling Financial Markets (MFM)'

Description: 'Sentence embeddings as features: first two principal components of MiniLM embeddings of financial tweets, and the learning curve of logistic regression on embeddings and on TF-IDF features.

The shaded learning-curve bands are the mean accuracy plus/minus two standard deviations across ten training resamples at each smaller training size, not confidence intervals for the population accuracy. At the full training sample there is one fit, so no resampling band is estimated. The TF-IDF learning curve uses min_df=1; the main classification comparison uses min_df=2. These are distinct feature filters, so their full-training-sample scores need not coincide.'

Keywords: 'sentence embeddings, MiniLM, TF-IDF, logistic regression, learning curve, principal components'

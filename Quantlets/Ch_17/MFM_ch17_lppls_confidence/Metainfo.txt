The circular-block confidence interval for the difference includes zero, while the HAC test is borderline. This does not establish that the indicator anticipates crashes. The 90-day forward windows overlap; signal dates are not independent prediction trials. Unfitted windows count as failures in the filter-frequency bars; those bars describe all candidate windows, not only successful fits.'

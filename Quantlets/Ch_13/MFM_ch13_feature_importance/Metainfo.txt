Their scales differ: impurity importance, decrease in ROC AUC (baseline minus permuted), and mean absolute contribution to the predicted probability. Bar lengths across panels are not interchangeable. The plotted MDA values are averages over permutations, without confidence intervals; the sign or size of a mean alone does not establish statistical significance.'
